              06 / How it works

              How it works

              Three small steps, and the list sorts itself.

              1. 01

                Pick your mood

                A date, a quiet desk, a garden, somewhere pretty or somewhere cheap. Choose one, or a few.

              2. 02

                Add your must-haves

                Good Wi-Fi, a river view, open late, room for the dog. You'll only see cafés that have every one you tick.

              3. 03

                Get cafés ranked for you

                Every café gets a match score from your picks, with a short note on why it fits.

              Must-haves are strict: a café without one of yours isn't shown. The rest are ranked by a custom match score. Your moods count the most, and being near a BTS or MRT station adds a little. A café that ticks every box gets close to 100%.

              Ask the barista is a chat built from simple word rules. It runs fully in your browser, with no AI service and no account, and your saved cafés stay on this device too.
